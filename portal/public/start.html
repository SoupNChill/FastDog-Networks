Your first website

Put your site on FastDog in 3 steps

Start with an address you own, then connect the server where your app runs.

Checking your progress…

  1. Add your website address

    Add the DNS records shown on the Website page and verify your address.

  2. Install the agent on the server where your app runs

    After your address is verified, follow the Server page instructions.

  3. Wait for the live check

    The Website page shows when your verified address has a connected agent. We then switch on HTTPS for it.

Keep your existing setup

One CNAME record points your address at FastDog. You also add a TXT record to prove you own it. Keep both records in place.

Your existing DNS provider, whether Cloudflare or another provider, stays where it is. Leave your other DNS records unchanged. The agent connects to your existing app; you do not need to move it or change its code.

Domains is optional - only if you want to buy a new domain.

Plan can wait - you can choose one later with support. Your account’s assigned plan and limits apply; billing is handled by the operator.
