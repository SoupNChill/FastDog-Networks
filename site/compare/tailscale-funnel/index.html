FastDog vs Tailscale Funnel

Tailscale Funnel shares a service on a device in your tailnet with the public internet. FastDog puts a self-hosted app on your own domain. They overlap for "make this app on my box reachable over HTTPS," and differ a lot after that.

Short version

  • Pick Tailscale Funnel if you already use Tailscale, a *.ts.net address is fine, and it’s personal or non-commercial use on the free Personal plan.
  • Pick FastDog if you have an ordinary HTTP(S) app (no WebSockets, no long or streaming requests, uploads under 50 MiB) and need it on your own domain, with your current DNS host, connected by your AI assistant.

Where Tailscale Funnel is better

  • Free for personal use, and it comes with a full private network (tailnet) and SSH.
  • End-to-end TLS to your device. Tailscale’s relays can’t read the traffic [1].
  • More protocols: a TCP forwarder for SSH, RDP, and other TCP services [3], where FastDog is HTTP(S) only.
  • Mature networking company, with a broad platform.
  • Great for sharing something quickly if you’re already on Tailscale. tailscale funnel 3000 is one command [1].

Where FastDog is different

  • Your own domain. Funnel is *.ts.net only [1].
  • Commercial use from $9/mo without per-user pricing.
  • No tailnet required. One agent container and two DNS records.
  • AI setup with safe keys. A 1-hour, connect-only token from the portal prompt, and account-scoped API keys on the trial and paid plans, and the agent key stays on your server.
