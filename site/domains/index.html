01 / Registration to HTTPS

A name with a route to your service.

  1. You tell us the name

    Send the domain you want to register and the service you want to put behind it.

  2. We register it in your name

    Registration is at cost. You are the registrant.

  3. We point a hostname at the edge

    Attach a hostname with a CNAME to attach.fastdognetworks.com, just like Edge.

  4. HTTPS

    You run the agent. We handle the edge TLS setup; requests reach your origin through the tunnel.
