Questions

What is FastDog?
FastDog puts your website behind our edge. We answer HTTPS for your hostname with a certificate we get for you, then pass each request to your app through a tunnel that your server opens to us. You don’t open ports or move your app.
How do I connect my app?
Create an account in the portal, add your hostname, add two DNS records, then run the connect command on the server where your app runs. The docs walk through each step.
Which DNS records do I need?
A CNAME on your hostname pointing at the target the portal shows, and a TXT record on _fastdog-challenge. plus your hostname that proves you control it. Keep both in place. On Cloudflare, set the CNAME to “DNS only”.
Do I have to move my domain or nameservers?
No. Your domain stays with your registrar and your DNS stays where it is. You only add the two records.
What do you log?
Your account email, IP addresses in rate limits and access logs, your hostnames, and request metadata such as time, path, status and user agent, which we use for status pages and bot scoring. We don’t store request or response bodies. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
Do you sell my data?
No. We don’t sell it or use it for advertising.
How much does it cost?
Plans are monthly in US dollars. See the pricing page for current plans and limits.
Is there an uptime guarantee?
No. We work hard to stay up, but there is no service level agreement. See the Terms of Service.
How do I delete my account?
Remove your CNAME records so traffic stops coming to us, then email support from your account’s email address and ask us to delete the account. We remove your hostnames, agent credentials and account details.
How do I get help?
Email support@example.invalid with your hostname and what you see. The portal footer also shows the support address.
