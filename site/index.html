01 / The problem

The distance between a domain and a working service should be short.

DNS, ingress, and certificates are often managed in separate places. When something breaks, that separation makes a simple request hard to trace. We bring those three pieces into one deliberate path.

02 / What we run

One path, three parts.

  • 01

    Authoritative DNS

    We serve the DNS records for your domain. You keep your registrar and control when to delegate nameservers.

  • 02

    Private tunnel

    An agent connects your origin to our edge, so the public request does not need a direct route to your service.

  • 03

    TLS reverse proxy

    The edge terminates HTTPS and forwards requests through the tunnel to the right origin.

03 / Who it's for

For teams that run their own services and want a smaller ingress surface.

Especially useful when the origin sits behind a private network or when you want one operator to own the DNS-to-TLS path. We run this architecture ourselves; we are opening it carefully to others.

05 / Cutover

Verify first. Delegate when ready.

We prepare the zone and verify answers directly on our nameserver IPs. When the nameserver cut is live and you choose to move, you will point your registrar at ns1.fastdognetworks.com and ns2.fastdognetworks.com. Your registrar remains yours.

06 / Cutover guarantee

Our promise.

  1. Same-business-day first reply (America/Denver). If we miss it, the first month is free.
  2. Guided cutover: after the customer points nameservers at FastDog and we have a working origin, if the site is not live on FastDog DNS + tunnel + TLS within 5 business days, setup is waived.
  3. If the cut fails, we help flip nameservers back and that month is free.

How to start: email support@fastdognetworks.com with domain, registrar, and where the origin lives.
