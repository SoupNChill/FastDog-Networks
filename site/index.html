01 / What we run

A name. A path. A decision.

Edge ↗

DNS + tunnel + TLS proxy. Start with a hostname attach; authoritative DNS is available when you want it.

The agent opens an outbound tunnel. The edge terminates HTTPS and forwards through it to your origin.

Domains ↗

Registered at cost, in your name, live behind FastDog the same day.

You are the registrant. Then attach a hostname to the edge.

Verdict ↗

Early access

Allow / challenge / block at the edge. A self-hosted proof-of-work challenge; fails open by default if the decision service is unavailable.

On our edge, decisions came back in under 7 ms at p99 at 200 requests per second.

02 / Who it's for

For teams that run their own services.

For origins behind a private network, and teams that want one operator to handle the DNS-to-TLS path. Start with a subdomain; keep your registrar and your current DNS host.

Our promise.
