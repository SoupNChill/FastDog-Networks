02 / Who it's for

For teams that run their own services.

For origins behind a private network, and teams that want one operator to handle the DNS-to-TLS path. Start with a subdomain; keep your registrar and your current DNS host.

03 / What you keep

What you keep control of.

  • Your registrar and your DNS

    Attach uses one CNAME at your current DNS host. Your domain stays registered where it is, and your other records stay as they are.

  • Your app and its ports

    Your service keeps running where it runs. The agent connects out to the edge, so you don't open inbound ports for it.

  • Detach any time

    Remove the CNAME and traffic for that hostname stops coming to FastDog. Tell us, and we remove the hostname and its agent credential on our side.

  • Only the hostname's edge

    FastDog handles TLS, the proxy, and the tunnel for the hostnames you attach.

Adding a hostname goes through us today: email support@fastdognetworks.com and we reply the same business day.
