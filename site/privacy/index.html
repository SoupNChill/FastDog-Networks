In short

We collect what we need to run the service and keep it safe. We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising.

1. What we collect

Account details
Your email address and a one-way hash of your password. We never store your password itself.
Terms acceptance (planned)
When open sign-up launches, we will record which version of these documents you accepted, when, and a salted hash of the IP address you used. Not the IP address itself. Sign-up does not record this yet.
IP addresses
Kept in rate limits (to stop abuse such as password guessing), in Verdict bot-decision logs, and in shortened form (last part removed) in web server access logs where we turn them on.
Hostnames
The hostnames you attach, their DNS check results, and the origin address your agent forwards to.
Request metadata
For bot scoring, Verdict looks at its IP address, network (ASN), path and headers such as the user agent. It stores only a decision log: time, IP address, path without the query string, and the decision. Web server access logs, where turned on, keep the method, path without the query string, status code, response time and a shortened IP address; headers and cookies are removed. Portal logs keep only the method, route pattern, status and response time. For API keys we keep an audit list of key ID, route pattern, method, status and time. We do not store request or response bodies, and we keep your email address out of logs.
Outgoing emails
Sign-in emails (address verification and password reset) are written to a private outbox on our server: your email address, the message type and the link.
Messages
Emails you send us and our replies.

2. Why we use it

  • To run the service: sign-in, routing your hostnames, issuing certificates, connecting your agent.
  • To keep it safe: rate limits, abuse prevention, and investigating phishing, malware or attacks.
  • To show you your status, traffic and bot scores.
  • To bill paid plans and answer support requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. How long we keep it

  • Account details and terms acceptance: while your account exists, then deleted, except where we must keep billing records by law.
  • Rate-limit entries: each one stops counting when its window ends, from one minute up to 24 hours for daily limits such as agent credential rotation. Ended entries are deleted the next time that limiter is used, so an idle entry can stay stored for longer.
  • Verdict decision logs: cleared once the oldest entry is 7 days old or the log reaches 5 MiB. The check runs when the next decision is written, so a log that gets no new traffic is not cleared on a timer.
  • Web server access logs: rolled over at 5 MiB; at most five old files are kept, and old files are removed after 7 days when the log next rolls over.
  • API key audit list: the latest 1,000 entries per account. It is limited by count, not by time.
  • Outbox emails: removed by hand by our operator. Automatic deletion is not built yet.
  • Guaranteed time-based deletion for all of the logs above is planned but not in place yet (TODO: set the exact periods before launch).
  • Email messages: as long as needed to handle your request.

4. Who processes it for us

We use a small number of providers to run FastDog: hosting providers for our servers and, once we connect one, an email provider for sign-in and service emails. Today sign-in emails go to the private outbox described above and are not sent through an email provider. When paid billing starts, a payment processor will handle card details; we will not see or store full card numbers. Each provider only gets the data it needs to do its job.

  • Payments: TODO, list when billing goes live.

5. No selling

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We disclose it only to the providers above, when the law requires it, or to protect people and the service from serious harm.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, export it, or delete it along with your account. Depending on where you live you may have further rights, including the right to complain to a data protection authority. We will verify the request comes from the account’s email address and reply within 30 days.

7. Security

We use HTTPS everywhere, hash passwords, API keys and agent credentials, keep secrets out of logs and limit who can reach production systems. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Each version has a version string at the top of this page. For material changes we will email account holders before they take effect.
